# Bags  x U.S. Black Chambers - Target Retail Readiness Accelerator | Partnership Proposal

## **A Strategic Partnership for Scalable Growth**

Bags is a modern technical assistance platform built to make small business financial data clear, actionable, and productive.

Our tools are designed to give small businesses and their stakeholders one place to drive progress across:

**1️⃣ Financial Hygiene**

**2️⃣ Financial Efficiency**

**3️⃣ Financial Growth**

## **Why This Matters for U.S Black Chambers**

Your mission is to support exceptional businesses, many of whom struggle with:

- A financial operations knowledge gap
- Incomplete or messy financials
- Limited access to non-dilutive capital
- Expensive equity raises or predatory loans to solve unnecessary cash flow problems

**Bags fixes this.**

By layering Bags into your support stack, your community gains:

✅ A foundational understanding of their financial hygiene

✅ A real-time understanding of the quality of their financial reporting

✅ CFO insights reports to make stronger growth decisions

✅ A financial dashboard to track their most important metrics

✅ An infrastructure to manage new and existing liabilities

✅ Non-dilutive capital access across a vetted network of mission-driven lending partners

**Our Platform is designed to be entrepreneur-first with contextualized education and clear next steps.**

## Proposed Pilot Program

Bags will work with USBC to provide a curriculum and support for a Finance and Access to Capital module as part of the Retail Readiness Accelerator program, in partnership with Target.

Each participant will receive one year of access to the Bags platform, access to our network of mission-driven lenders, monthly cohort education sessions, and personal capital readiness advisory from our Financing and Access to Capital specialists.
