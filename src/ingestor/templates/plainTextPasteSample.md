# Executive Summary

Bags proposes a multi-year strategic partnership with the National Urban League to deploy an AI-powered financial platform to entrepreneurs across the National Urban League ecosystem.

This partnership will:

- Position the Urban League at the forefront of AI adoption for Black entrepreneurs
- Give 10,000 entrepreneurs an end-to-end tool for financial growth
- Create a pathway from financial readiness to capital access via the Urban Empowerment Fund
- Deliver real-time data and impact reporting across the network
- Generate a fundable, multi-year program that attracts institutional sponsors

## The Opportunity

For over a century, the National Urban League has been a pillar for economic empowerment and entrepreneurship support. Access to capital remains the number one challenge these businesses face.

Simultaneously, AI is transforming how businesses operate. Without intentional intervention, Black entrepreneurs risk being left behind in this technological shift.

## National Urban League Strategic Overview

- Founded 1910, 111+ years serving urban communities
- Network of 90+ affiliates across 35+ states
- CDFI operations provide capital access

## Partnership Structure - $5M Program

| Component |  | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NUL National Capacity Building & Technical Assistance | Business advisory services and capital readiness support | $1,250,720 |
| NUL National Affiliate Activation | Sub-grants to select affiliates for local implementation support | $350,000 |
| UEF Catalytic Capital Program | Performance-based or milestone-driven grants to participating businesses | $500,000 |
| Bags Technology Infrastructure | 12-month access to Bags for 10,000 businesses | $1,799,280 |
| Total Program Cost |  | $5,000,000 |

## Key Value Propositions

- Scalable Technology: Platform designed for impact at scale
- Proven Track Record: Established partnerships with mission-driven institutions
- Comprehensive Impact Measurement: Network-wide analytics and reporting
- Low cost per business served: ~$224.93 per year for Bags

## The Bags Solution: Validated Financial Data at Scale

Our platform delivers three integrated capabilities:

1. Financial Hygiene: AI-powered diagnostic validates accounting data
2. Financial Efficiency: CFO-level insights with projections and KPI tracking
3. Financial Growth: Custom debt strategy with viable loan calculations

## Why This Partnership Accelerates NUL Impact

- Scale Without Complexity: Deploy across 90+ affiliates with unified platform and training
- Data-Driven Impact: Real-time visibility into outcomes and performance
- Foundation-Ready Reporting: Structured data for funders and the public
- Revenue Quality Improvement: Historical users show 250% YoY revenue growth
